PRIVACY POLICY

When you contact Ambica Machine Tools or interact with our website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Full name

  • Email address

  • Business or correspondence address

  • Technical specifications and project requirements
  • Enquiry details and messages
  • Information relating to quotations, purchase orders, or business requirements
  • Information provided for customer support or after-sales service

This information may be used for website security, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, analytics, and improving our website and services.

Ambica Machine Tools may use the information collected for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

We will generally use personal information for purposes reasonably connected with our business activities and your interaction with Ambica Machine Tools.

Ambica Machine Tools takes reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Ambica Machine Tools does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

We may disclose or share relevant information with trusted third parties where reasonably necessary to operate our business, provide products or services, or comply with legal requirements.

These parties may include:

  • Professional advisers, consultants, or auditors where reasonably required
  • Banks, payment service providers, or financial institutions where applicable

Where information is shared with service providers, we seek to limit the information provided to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor behaviour, remember preferences, analyze website traffic, maintain website security, and improve the overall user experience.

Cookies are small data files that may be stored on your device through your web browser.

You may be able to disable, restrict, or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality, availability, or performance of some portions of our website.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, applications, social media platforms, or other external services for your convenience or reference.

Ambica Machine Tools is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third-party websites or services.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including where necessary for:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Preparing quotations and proposals
  • Providing products and services
  • Processing and maintaining business transactions
  • Maintaining customer, supplier, and business records
  • Providing technical and after-sales support
  • Meeting legal, accounting, tax, or regulatory requirements
  • Resolving disputes

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we may securely delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, and legitimate business requirements.
