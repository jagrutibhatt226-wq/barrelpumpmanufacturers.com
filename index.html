Barrel Pump Types

Types of Industrial Barrel Pumps

Different industrial liquid transfer requirements can require different barrel pump drive and construction arrangements.

01

Electric Barrel Pump

Electric motor-operated barrel pumps are suitable where powered and convenient liquid transfer is required from compatible drums and containers.

02

Pneumatic Barrel Pump

Pneumatic barrel pumps use compressed air as the drive source and can be selected for suitable industrial environments and liquid transfer duties.

03

Rotary & Manual Barrel Pump

Rotary hand-operated barrel pumps provide a simple manual solution for transferring compatible liquids from drums and suitable containers.

04

Piston Barrel Pump

Piston type barrel pumps are designed for suitable manual or pneumatic liquid transfer applications.

05

Screw Type Barrel Pump

Screw type barrel pump configurations can be used where a suitable controlled transfer arrangement is required.

06

Drum Pump

Drum pumps are used to transfer compatible liquids directly from drums, barrels and other suitable containers.
