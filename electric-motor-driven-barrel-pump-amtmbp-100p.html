Product Overview

PP / PVDF Electric Drum Transfer Pump

The AMTMBP-100P electric barrel pump is designed for applications that require transferring compatible liquids from drums or suitable containers to another process point. Depending on the selected configuration, material options include polypropylene (PP), PVDF and Teflon components.

The available product listing states an approximate open-discharge flow capacity of 80–90 litres per minute and a maximum head of 15 metres. Actual performance depends on liquid properties, delivery-line dimensions, elevation, fittings and system conditions.

Before selecting this industrial barrel pump, identify the liquid, its concentration, temperature and viscosity, the required delivery rate and the electrical supply available at the installation site. Confirm that the selected materials and seals are suitable for the intended application.
