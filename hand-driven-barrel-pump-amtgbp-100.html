Product Overview

Aluminum Hand Rotary Drum Pump

The AMTGBP-100 is a hand rotary driven barrel pump designed for transferring suitable liquids from compatible drums and containers. The listed material of construction is aluminum, and the rotary mechanism is operated manually.

The available product listing states an approximate output of 100 litres per minute and a listed head of 15 metres.
