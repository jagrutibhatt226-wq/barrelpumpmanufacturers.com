Product Overview

Polypropylene Hand Rotary Drum Transfer Pump

The AMTBPP-100 Hand Rotary Driven Barrel Pump is designed for manual transfer of compatible liquids from suitable drums and containers. The pump is operated by turning its rotary handle, so an electric motor or compressed-air supply is not required for operation.

The listed approximate output is 1 litre per 3–4 rotations, and the pump is specified for suitable 5–55 gallon drums. Actual output can vary depending on the liquid properties and operating conditions.

Polypropylene is the specified pump material. Before using the pump with chemicals, oils or solvents, confirm compatibility with the liquid, its concentration and temperature, as well as the complete pump assembly.
