Product Overview

Electric Drum Transfer Pump for Industrial Use

The AMTMBP-100S electric barrel pump is designed for applications that require liquid to be transferred from a drum or compatible container to another process point. Its SS316 construction makes it a candidate for selected industrial duties where stainless steel wetted parts are specified.

With an approximate open-discharge flow rating of 80–90 litres per minute and a listed maximum head of 15 metres, this model can be evaluated for suitable liquid-transfer operations. Actual performance varies with liquid properties, delivery-line dimensions, elevation, fittings and other system conditions.

Before selecting a pump, identify the liquid being handled, its temperature and viscosity, the required delivery rate and the electrical supply available at the installation site. These details help establish whether this configuration is appropriate for the intended duty.
