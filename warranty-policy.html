PRODUCT WARRANTY POLICY

AMBICA MACHINE TOOLS

Industrial Pumps | High Pressure Valves & Fittings | Hydro Testing Equipment | Seawater Desalination Systems

Effective Date: September 2026

Ambica Machine Tools is committed to supplying reliable industrial pumping, high-pressure flow-control and water-treatment solutions. We manufacture and supply a wide range of industrial pumps, high-pressure valves and fittings, hydro-testing equipment and seawater desalination systems from our manufacturing facility in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

Our products are subjected to appropriate quality and inspection procedures before dispatch. Our quality practices include checks relating to pressure performance, capacity, material quality, dimensional accuracy, durability, finish and other applicable product parameters.

This Product Warranty Policy establishes the standard warranty terms applicable to products manufactured and/or supplied by Ambica Machine Tools.

1. Standard Warranty

Unless a different warranty period is specifically stated in our quotation, purchase order acceptance, product documentation, or written agreement, Ambica Machine Tools warrants that its manufactured products will be free from defects in material and workmanship under normal and intended operating conditions.

Standard Warranty Period

12 months from the date of installation OR 18 months from the date of invoice, whichever occurs earlier.

The warranty applies only to manufacturing defects and workmanship defects attributable to Ambica Machine Tools.

For products supplied with a specific warranty period stated in the quotation, order confirmation, technical specification, or product documentation, that specific warranty period shall prevail.

2. Scope of Warranty

During the applicable warranty period, if a product is proven to have a manufacturing or workmanship defect covered under this warranty, Ambica Machine Tools may, at its discretion:

  • Repair the defective product or component;
  • Replace the defective component with a new or reconditioned component;
  • Replace the product where repair is not technically or commercially practical; or
  • Provide an appropriate corrective solution after technical evaluation.

The decision regarding repair, replacement of components, or other corrective action shall be made by Ambica Machine Tools after inspection and technical assessment.

Replacement parts supplied under warranty will generally be covered only for the remaining period of the original product warranty, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

3. Conditions for Warranty Applicability

The warranty shall apply only when the product:

  • Has been installed correctly and in accordance with the applicable manufacturer's instructions;
  • Has been operated within the specified pressure, temperature, flow, speed, capacity, voltage, air pressure and other operating limits;
  • Has been used with the specified or suitable operating medium;
  • Has been properly maintained and serviced;
  • Has not been modified, altered, dismantled, repaired or re-engineered by an unauthorized person;
  • Has not been subjected to abnormal operating conditions, excessive loading, pressure surges, overheating, dry running, cavitation, contamination or other unsuitable conditions; and
  • Has been used for the application for which it was designed and supplied.

5. Important Warranty Exclusions

The warranty does not cover failures, damage or deterioration resulting from:

A. Normal Wear and Tear

Normal wear and tear of consumable or service parts is not considered a manufacturing defect.

Depending on the product, such parts may include:

  • Mechanical seals
  • O-rings
  • Gaskets
  • Diaphragms
  • Packing
  • Valve seats
  • PTFE components
  • Rubber components
  • Hoses
  • Flexible connections
  • Bearings
  • Belts
  • Filters
  • Other consumable or wear components

Such components may require periodic replacement depending on operating conditions.

B. Incorrect Installation

Warranty does not cover damage resulting from:

  • Incorrect installation
  • Incorrect alignment
  • Improper piping
  • Incorrect electrical connections
  • Improper foundation or mounting
  • Incorrect suction conditions
  • Improper pressure connections
  • Failure to follow installation instructions

C. Incorrect Operation

Warranty does not cover damage resulting from:

  • Operation above the rated pressure
  • Operation above the rated temperature
  • Excessive speed
  • Excessive flow
  • Dry running where the product is not designed for dry running
  • Cavitation
  • Pressure shock or sudden pressure surge
  • Improper air pressure
  • Overloading
  • Operation outside specified duty conditions
  • Operation with unsuitable fluids or chemicals

D. External or Accidental Damage

The warranty does not cover damage caused by:

  • Impact
  • Dropping
  • Transportation after delivery
  • Mishandling
  • Accident
  • Fire
  • Flood
  • Lightning
  • Natural disasters
  • Corrosive external environments
  • Improper storage
  • Unauthorized modification

Visible physical damage, impact damage, broken components, cracked bodies, bent shafts, damaged connections or similar damage caused after delivery shall not normally be considered manufacturing defects.

E. Unauthorized Repair or Modification

Warranty becomes void for the affected product or component where unauthorized persons have:

  • Opened or dismantled the equipment;
  • Modified the product;
  • Changed original components;
  • Altered pressure settings;
  • Changed the design;
  • Performed unauthorized repairs; or
  • Used non-approved replacement parts where such use caused the failure.

6. High-Pressure Products – Special Warranty Conditions

High-pressure pumps, valves, fittings and hydro-testing equipment must be operated strictly within their specified pressure and temperature ratings.

The customer/user is responsible for ensuring that:

  • The correct pressure rating is selected for the application;
  • All connected hoses, tubes, fittings and accessories have suitable pressure ratings;
  • The equipment is installed correctly;
  • Appropriate safety procedures are followed;
  • Pressure is not increased beyond the rated working pressure;
  • The equipment is inspected and maintained appropriately.

A failure of an external hose, pipe, fitting, connection or accessory supplied by another manufacturer shall not automatically constitute a warranty defect in the Ambica product.

Where a high-pressure product is returned for warranty evaluation, Ambica Machine Tools reserves the right to inspect and test the product before determining warranty applicability.

7. Pump Warranty – Special Conditions

Pump performance depends on proper installation, suction conditions, operating medium, piping, alignment, operating pressure, temperature, speed and maintenance.

The warranty does not cover pump failure caused by:

  • Dry running
  • Cavitation
  • Improper suction conditions
  • Blockage in suction/discharge piping
  • Operation with unsuitable liquid
  • Abrasive or contaminated liquid beyond the specified application
  • Incorrect motor/engine operation
  • Incorrect rotation
  • Excessive pressure
  • Excessive temperature
  • Lack of lubrication
  • Improper alignment
  • Unauthorized modification or repair

Where a pump is returned with a reported defect, Ambica Machine Tools may inspect the pump and determine whether the issue is attributable to manufacturing/workmanship or to operating, installation, maintenance or external conditions.

8. Valve & Fitting Warranty

Valves and fittings are warranted against manufacturing and workmanship defects during the applicable warranty period.

Warranty does not cover:

  • Damage caused by excessive pressure
  • Incorrect installation
  • Incorrect tightening
  • Thread damage caused during installation
  • Improper tools or handling
  • Chemical incompatibility
  • Corrosion caused by unsuitable service conditions
  • Normal seat, packing or seal wear
  • Damage caused by unauthorized dismantling or modification
  • Damage caused by external impact

For high-pressure valves, the customer must ensure that the complete connected system, including tubing, hose, fittings and accessories, is rated appropriately for the intended pressure and application.

9. Seawater Desalination System Warranty

For seawater desalination and RO systems, warranty coverage applies to the equipment and components specifically covered in the quotation/order documentation.

Performance of a desalination system depends on feed-water quality, temperature, salinity, pressure, pre-treatment, operating procedures and regular maintenance.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, the following may be treated as consumable/service items and may be excluded from the standard equipment warranty:

  • RO membranes
  • Cartridge filters
  • Filter elements
  • Seals and O-rings
  • Chemical dosing consumables
  • Other consumable items

RO membrane performance is dependent on actual feed-water conditions and proper pretreatment and operation; therefore, membrane replacement or performance deterioration shall be assessed based on operating data and the applicable system specification.

10. Third-Party Components

Where a product incorporates components manufactured by third parties, such as motors, engines, electrical equipment, instruments, gauges, sensors, controllers, batteries, electronic components or other purchased items, warranty coverage may be subject to the original manufacturer's warranty terms.

Ambica Machine Tools may assist the customer in coordinating a warranty claim with the relevant manufacturer or supplier where applicable.

11. Warranty Claim Procedure

To make a warranty claim, the customer should contact Ambica Machine Tools as soon as the suspected defect is identified.

The customer may be requested to provide:

  • Invoice or order reference
  • Product model and serial number, where applicable
  • Installation date
  • Description of the problem
  • Operating conditions
  • Photographs and/or videos
  • Test reports or operating data, where applicable
  • Maintenance records, where relevant

For technical evaluation, Ambica Machine Tools may request that the product or affected component be returned to our works.

The customer should not dismantle, repair, modify or dispose of the affected component before receiving instructions from Ambica Machine Tools, particularly where warranty evaluation is required.

12. Inspection and Warranty Determination

All warranty claims are subject to technical inspection and verification.

After inspection, Ambica Machine Tools will determine whether the reported failure is:

A. Covered under warranty, or

B. Not covered under warranty.

If the failure is determined to be a manufacturing or workmanship defect covered under this policy, the applicable corrective action will be provided in accordance with Section 2.

If the failure is determined to be outside warranty coverage, Ambica Machine Tools may provide a repair or replacement quotation to the customer, where technically possible.

Repair or replacement of a product or component under warranty shall not automatically restart or extend the original warranty period unless specifically agreed in writing.

The repaired or replacement component shall generally remain covered only for the balance of the original applicable warranty period.
