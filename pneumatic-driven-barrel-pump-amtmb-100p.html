Product Overview

PP / PVDF Pneumatic Barrel Pump for Industrial Use

The AMTMB-100P pneumatic barrel pump is designed for applications that require compatible liquids to be transferred from a drum or suitable container to another process point. Its available polypropylene (PP), PVDF and Teflon material options can be considered according to the fluid and operating requirements.

The supplied product listing specifies an approximate flow capacity of 80–90 litres per minute and a listed head of 15 metres. Actual performance depends on liquid properties, air supply, delivery-line dimensions, elevation, fittings and other system conditions.

Before selecting a pump, identify the liquid being handled, its temperature and viscosity, the required delivery rate and the available compressed-air supply. Confirm chemical compatibility and operating parameters with the manufacturer.
